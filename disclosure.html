Affiliate disclosure

We believe you should know when a link may financially support this website.

How affiliate links work

FindoraDaily may participate in affiliate programs in the future. When we do, clicking certain clearly identified links and completing qualifying purchases may result in a commission to us, without increasing your purchase price. Eligibility, attribution, and commission terms depend on the seller and affiliate program.

Current status

As of this initial website release, no affiliate links or commercial partnerships are active on this site.

How links will be labeled

Each relevant article or recommendation will carry a clear, conspicuous disclosure near affiliate links, such as: “This post contains affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you purchase through them.” This page is supplementary, not a substitute for an article-level disclosure.

Editorial approach

We do not promise that an affiliate link represents the lowest price or best item for every buyer. Prices and availability can change. Product information must be checked against current, permitted sources before publishing. We will not invent first-hand experiences, purchases, testing, or endorsements.
