Our approach

About FindoraDaily

FindoraDaily is an independent, early-stage product discovery project focused on practical buying guidance.

Why we're building it

Shopping recommendations can be confusing. Our goal is to create simple guides that explain what a product is for, who might benefit, and what to consider before spending money.

Our editorial principles

  • Make clear what has—and has not—been independently verified.
  • Never claim hands-on testing or customer experiences that did not occur.
  • Differentiate factual product information from opinions or estimates.
  • Disclose commercial relationships near recommendations and on our disclosure page.
  • Correct mistakes and remove outdated claims when identified.

Current status

We are preparing the website and research process. There are no sponsored listings or published product reviews at launch. Affiliate partnerships, if established later, will be disclosed.
